## 🚨 The Problem
When municipal infrastructure fails—power cuts, dry taps, and voltage surges—neighborhood communication instantly collapses into chaos. Local WhatsApp groups flood with hundreds of panicked duplicate messages, frantic demands for updates, and unverified reference numbers. Citizens are left completely in the dark, while municipalities struggle with fragmented, uncoordinated reports.

---

## 💡 The Solution: ResiGrid
**ResiGrid** replaces the panic with precision. It is an end-to-end municipal resilience platform that bridges the gap between citizens and city depots. By combining automated **ESP32 hardware sensors**, a **10km spatial radar**, **smart report deduplication**, and **live municipal dispatch**, ResiGrid turns infrastructure breakdown into total clarity.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

* **📡 Live 10km Spatial Radar:** Real-time visual mapping of power outages and water pressure drops across your neighborhood.
* **⚡ Hardware-Triggered Alerts:** Embedded ESP32 sensors automatically detect micro-voltage sags and line trips *before* appliances get fried.
* **🧩 Smart 50-Meter Deduplication:** Automatically groups identical neighborhood reports into single, high-priority municipal tickets—ending chat spam and ticket flooding.
* **🤝 P2P Mutual Aid & Spaza Clearance:** Neighbors can coordinate shared power/water nodes, while local spaza shops list discounted clearance food before stock spoils during extended outages.
* **⛅ Climate & Weather Integration:** Real-time severe weather and storm alerts (SAWS compatible) pushed directly to local zones.
* **🚚 Municipal Dispatch Portal:** City depots can assign field crews, track SLAs live on the map, and automatically push WhatsApp status updates back to residents.
* **📊 Usage & Savings Calculators:** Built-in household utilities tracking to measure water and energy conservation during crises.

---

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

* **Frontend / Mobile App:** Next.js, Capacitor
* **IoT Hardware Layer:** ESP32 Microcontrollers with voltage/pressure sensor modules
* **Backend & Automation:** Supabase / Cloud Functions / Automated WhatsApp Webhooks
* **Design System:** Custom dark-mode UI with electric turquoise (`#00E5FF`) and charcoal styling
